# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming learning community.

## Expected Behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Teach and learn in good faith.
- Give fair feedback and honest skill claims.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or hate speech.
- Spamming, scam behavior, or exploitation of users.
- Sharing private data without consent.

## Enforcement
Moderators may remove content, mute users, or suspend accounts for violations.
